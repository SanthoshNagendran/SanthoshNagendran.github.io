04 · Production engineering

Two chips, one click, about 30 seconds.

A charger has two processors: an AVR on the power board and an ESP32 on the IoT board. My flashing tool programs both on a vendor fixture and gives the operator a single PASS / FAIL. Press play to replay a real run from the log.

0.0 s
Cycle progressready
Measured on the line: 31.3 – 31.9 s per board pair. Most of it is writing the 1.88 MB ESP32 application.
